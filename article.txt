Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the way people work, learn, and communicate.
Students now use AI to summarize notes, generate ideas, and understand difficult topics faster.
Businesses use AI to automate repetitive tasks, analyze customer feedback, and improve decision making.

Python is one of the most popular programming languages for AI development because it has powerful libraries and a simple syntax.
Machine learning, natural language processing, and computer vision are some of the major fields of AI.

Learning AI requires patience and continuous practice.
Building small projects regularly is one of the best ways to improve programming skills.
Making mistakes, debugging code, and solving problems are all part of becoming a better developer.
